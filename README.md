<h1 align="center">BoofVision</h1>

<p align="center">Stream your game to your friends, pop each of their streams into its own window, and draw on a stream to point things out.<br>
Discord stays your voice chat; BoofVision carries the video.</p>

---

> **Beta.** Built by [Esteef.Ai](https://github.com/Esteef-Ai) for the Boof Squad. You need a BoofVision server
> address and group password to use it; Boof Squad members can ask for them in Discord.

## Download

**[⬇ BoofVision for Windows](https://github.com/Esteef-Ai/boofvision/releases/latest/download/BoofVision-portable.exe)**:
Windows 10 or 11, about 110 MB. There's nothing to install; it runs from wherever you save it.

The app isn't code-signed, so the first time you run it Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**.
Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Using it

1. Open BoofVision. Type the server, group password and room, add your name, and press **Join room**.
2. **Go live** and choose what to share:
   - **Windows**: one app only, such as your game. The rest of your screen stays private.
   - **Screens**: a whole monitor.
3. Friends' streams show up as tiles. **Pop out** (or double-click) gives a stream its own resizable window.
   In a pop-out, <kbd>T</kbd> pins it on top, <kbd>F</kbd> makes it fullscreen, <kbd>M</kbd> mutes it and
   <kbd>B</kbd> sends it back to the grid. Pop-outs come back where you left them.
4. **Let viewers draw on my stream** lets friends **right-click and drag** on your stream to circle or point at
   things, or right-click once to ping a spot. Everyone sees the marks, and so do you, on your own screen over the
   game. They fade after a few seconds.

**Tip:** leave *Share system audio* off while you're in a Discord call. It sends everything you hear, so friends
would hear their own voices echo.
